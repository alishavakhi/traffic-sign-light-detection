## 2. Traffic sign dataset — attribution incomplete

**Used by:** `src/signs_to_yolo.py`

The traffic sign half of the training set comes from a text-annotated detection
dataset (image files alongside a single `annotations.txt` of
`<image>:<record>;<record>;…` lines, with `MISC_SIGNS` used for unusable records).

**The original project did not record which dataset this is**, and it is not
identifiable from the annotation format alone. It is therefore **not** named here
rather than guessed at, and its licence terms are unknown.

Consequences:

- the converter in `src/signs_to_yolo.py` is original code written against the
  observed format, and makes no claim over the dataset;
- **no images from this dataset should be redistributed until its identity and
  licence are established** — see section 5;
- if you are the author of the original project and you know the source, replace
  this section with the dataset name, URL and licence.

and demonstrate the detector, at their native resolutions:

- **1280 × 720** frames are from the **Bosch Small Traffic Lights Dataset**, which

## 7. Trained model

The detector is a fine-tuned `yolo11n` (YOLO11 nano), starting from Ultralytics
COCO-pretrained weights and trained on the merged dataset. Model weights are **not**
included in this repository — they live on Roboflow, and the recorded sample
predictions were produced by querying that hosted model.

If you retrain, the resulting weights are your responsibility with respect to the
licences in sections 1, 2 and 3.
